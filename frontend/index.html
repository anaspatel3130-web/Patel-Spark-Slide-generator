Patel'Spark User Guide
STEP-BY-STEP OPERATION MANUAL

Welcome to Patel'Spark! Follow this step-by-step walkthrough to transform any business Excel workbook into high-impact, management-ready PowerPoint presentations and formatted audit workbooks.

1

Authentication & Platform Access

Click "Sign In" in the top navigation bar and enter your authorized account credentials to access the presentation studio.

💡 New Users: Click "Request Access" to submit your operational details. Once approved by an administrator, activate your account using your single-use invitation token.
2

Upload Any Excel Workbook (.xlsx / .xls)

Drag and drop your spreadsheet into the dropzone or click "Browse Local File". You can also click "Load Global Express Sample" or "Load Route Logistics Sample" for instant real-world test data.

🔍 Schema-Agnostic: Patel'Spark automatically detects multiple worksheets, merged cells, header rows, numerical metrics, origin-transit-destination lanes, flight schedules, customer accounts, and operational remarks!
3

Select Presentation Depth & Customization Options

Choose your desired presentation style:

  • Presentation Detail Mode: Select "Management Summary" for executive 2–6 slide overviews, or "Complete Data Preservation" to embed every spreadsheet row across paginated 10-row slide tables without font compression.
  • Slide Count: Choose "Auto (Adaptive)" (the engine determines the optimal length based on your data volume) or select a fixed length of 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6 slides.
  • Content Toggles: Enable/disable native charts, routing flow diagrams (Origin ➔ Transit ➔ Destination), and operational exceptions/remarks.
4

Preview 16:9 Slides & Inspect Data Integrity

Click "Preview Slide Structure" to open the interactive thumbnail carousel. Verify slide sequence, verified metrics, routing flow diagrams, and data provenance badges (Original Source Data vs. Deterministic Calculation).

5

Generate, Validate & Download Dual Outputs

Click "Generate Presentation & Formatted Excel". Watch the animated synthesis timeline, then download both files:

  • PowerPoint (.pptx): Approved 16:9 corporate presentation with official logos, per-slide watermarks, and native charts.
  • Formatted Excel (.xlsx): Retains 100% of your original worksheets and cell values unaltered with corporate styling and an Executive Summary audit sheet.
6

Cancel or Delete Generated Decks Anytime

After downloading, click "🗑️ Cancel & Delete Deck" in the results banner or use the "🗑️ Delete" button in the generation history table to permanently remove files and history records from storage.
